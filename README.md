# 🍱 TiffinTrail

### Ghar Jaisa Khana, Aapke Paas

TiffinTrail is a responsive UI/UX design concept for discovering local homemade tiffin services. It helps students, working professionals, bachelors, and families find fresh homemade food prepared by local home chefs.

---

## 📌 About the Project

**TiffinTrail** is a UI/UX design project created in Figma to make finding and ordering homemade food simple and convenient.

The platform allows users to discover nearby home chefs, explore tiffin menus, compare prices and ratings, and place a tiffin order.

The design focuses on a warm homemade-food experience, responsive layouts, simple navigation, accessibility, and user-friendly interaction.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Create a simple homemade food discovery experience.
- Help users find local tiffin services easily.
- Allow users to explore nearby home chefs.
- Display menus, prices, ratings, and delivery information.
- Provide a simple tiffin ordering experience.
- Create responsive designs for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile.
- Maintain consistent colors, typography, spacing, and components.
- Promote the concept of local homemade food.

## 📱 Responsive Design

The TiffinTrail design is created for three different screen sizes:

1. **Desktop — 1440 × 1024**
2. **Tablet — 768 × 1024**
3. **Mobile — 390 × 844**

The layouts are adapted for each screen size instead of simply scaling down the desktop design.

## 🎨 Design System

### Primary Colors

- **Terracotta / Warm Orange** — Homemade food & warmth
- **Leaf Green** — Freshness & healthy food
- **Cream / Off White** — Warm background
